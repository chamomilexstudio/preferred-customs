How to Order

From your idea to your finished model. Every project is different, so the details, price, and timeline are confirmed with you before work begins.

01

Share Your Idea

Tell us the vehicle, department, unit number, preferred scale, and any special details. Reference photographs are welcome.

02

Review Your Quote

We'll discuss feasibility, customization options, estimated cost, and expected turnaround before you commit.

03

Confirm Your Order

Once the details and price are agreed, we'll explain the deposit (collected through PayPal), payment schedule, and next steps. The balance is due when the model is complete.

04

Complete & Ship

We finish your model, confirm completion, and ship it to your address anywhere in the United States.

What to Expect

Straight answers before you spend anything.

How much does it cost?

Quotes depend on the vehicle, scale, complexity of the markings, and materials. You'll get a price before any deposit is requested.

How much is the deposit?

The deposit amount is confirmed with your quote. Nothing is charged until you've agreed to the details.

How long will it take?

Turnaround depends on the build. We'll give you a realistic estimate with your quote.

Can I approve the design?

Yes, we'll talk through the details with you before work begins. Send reference photos to help us get the markings right.

What if something goes wrong?

Cancellation, refund, and damage policies are explained when your quote is confirmed, so you know them before paying a deposit.

How is it shipped?

Models are carefully packaged and shipped within the United States. Shipping charges and tracking are included in your quote discussion.

Preferred Customs is an independent model builder and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any fire or police department.
